01The mark

Seven bricks in running bond, from round 1. The centre brick is the pane that has focus, with a >_ prompt on it: burnt darker (klinkkeri) with a white prompt on light grounds, lighter clay (savi) with a black prompt on dark ones. Below 64 px the prompt is left off; the brick is too small to hold it. Beside the wordmark the mark stands free. The app icon is a close-up of it on a kalkki tile in light mode and a koski tile in dark mode: the focus brick fills the tile's width with its prompt at the left, so the >_ stays legible at every size.

06App visual basis

The window from round 2, now in both themes. Panes sit on a mortar-coloured background, so the 3 px gaps read as mortar joints. The focused pane gets a brick line on top and a solid brick cursor; the others get an outlined cursor and dim slightly.

Pane anatomy

  • gap3 px, --term-gap. Dark mortar in dark mode, light mortar in light mode.
  • focus2 px brick line on the pane's top edge plus a solid brick cursor. No glow, no border around the whole pane.
  • inactiveOutlined cursor, pane at 82% opacity. Output keeps streaming.
  • projectA tint from the project list colours the pane's title chip and the cursor outline when grouping by repo is on.
  • title barBrick glyph, then app name and pane count, in Geist Mono 11.
