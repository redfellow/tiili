tiili · round 1 · ten small drafts

Red brick, Geist, and a grid of terminals

Each draft takes one thing from Tampere's old mill district: running bond, the arched Finlayson windows, painted factory signs, maker's stamps, the chimneys, ivy in autumn, the rapids at night. Pick two or three by number and I'll take them to full size.
